Privacy Policy

Huli Mane Ata (ಹುಲಿ ಮನೆ ಆಟ)

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Huli Mane Ata mobile application (the “App”), a traditional Tigers and Goats strategy board game, handles information. The App is designed to work offline and does not require an account.

1. Information the App stores

The App stores the following game-related information locally on your device so that its features work:

  • Game preferences, including selected game mode, board preset, AI difficulty, language, sound, haptic feedback and voice-guidance settings.
  • Player names that you enter for the Goat and Tiger sides.
  • Local gameplay statistics and match history, including match time, result, selected preset and mode, and the number of goats captured.

This information is kept in the App's private on-device storage. The App does not send it to the developer or to a developer-operated server. Android app backup is disabled for the App. Your information may be erased if you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.

2. Information collection, sharing and advertising

The current version of the App does not request an account, operate a backend, or include advertising, analytics or third-party database SDKs. The App does not collect or share personal information with the developer. It does not contain in-app chat, public profiles or user-generated content.

The App does not request network, location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. It requests Android's vibration permission only for optional haptic feedback.

3. Optional text-to-speech

If you enable voice guidance, the App asks the Android text-to-speech service installed on your device to speak short, predefined game instructions and game-result phrases in the selected language. The App does not record your voice or use the microphone. Text-to-speech processing is provided by the speech engine configured on your device. Depending on that engine and your device settings, speech processing may be performed on-device or by the engine provider. Please review the privacy information for your selected text-to-speech engine.

4. Children's privacy

The App does not ask users to provide personal information to the developer and does not include chat or advertising in the current version. The App's target age groups and availability for children are determined by the declarations made in Google Play Console. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of devices and review the device's text-to-speech settings. If the App's features or data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

5. Data retention and deletion

Game preferences, player names, statistics and match history remain on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. The App does not maintain a developer-side copy of this information and therefore has no server-side account or gameplay data to delete.

6. Security

Game information is stored in the App's private storage area provided by Android. No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free. Keep your device operating system up to date and use the device's available security controls.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App's functionality, dependencies or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the current version took effect. Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy published at this URL.

8. Contact

For questions or privacy-related requests about Huli Mane Ata, contact the developer using the support email address shown in the “App support” or “Developer contact” section of the Huli Mane Ata Google Play Store listing.
